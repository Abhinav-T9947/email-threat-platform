# MailSentinel Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 2026

MailSentinel ("MailSentinel", "we", "our", or "us") is an email threat analysis and forensic integrity platform. This Privacy Policy explains what information MailSentinel processes, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

## 1. Information We Process

When you use MailSentinel, we may process:

* Gmail account information provided through Google OAuth, such as your Google account identifier and email address.
* Email messages that you choose to analyze, including email headers, sender and recipient information, subject lines, message content, URLs, authentication information, relay information, IP addresses, and attachment information.
* Technical information required to operate and secure the service.
* Forensic case metadata, including cryptographic hashes and blockchain transaction information.

MailSentinel does not intentionally store the complete contents of analyzed emails in its forensic case database.

## 2. Gmail Access

MailSentinel uses Google OAuth to connect to Gmail.

Gmail access is requested with read-only permissions. MailSentinel uses this access to search for and retrieve email messages that you choose to analyze.

MailSentinel does not use Gmail access to send, modify, or delete your emails.

You can disconnect your Gmail account from MailSentinel at any time.

Disconnecting MailSentinel does not automatically revoke the application's authorization from your Google Account. You can revoke third-party application access through your Google Account settings.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information processed by MailSentinel is used to:

* Analyze emails for potentially malicious or suspicious characteristics.
* Perform forensic and threat analysis.
* Generate forensic reports and integrity information.
* Associate saved forensic cases with the authenticated MailSentinel user.
* Maintain the security and functionality of the platform.
* Provide blockchain-based integrity verification for forensic records.

MailSentinel does not use analyzed email content for advertising.

## 4. Forensic and Blockchain Records

MailSentinel generates cryptographic hashes for forensic evidence and reports.

Certain integrity information may be recorded on a blockchain. Blockchain records are designed to be immutable. As a result, deleting a forensic case from MailSentinel's database does not erase information that has already been written to a blockchain.

Blockchain records should therefore be considered permanent once created.

## 5. Storage and Security

MailSentinel stores account and forensic information in a database.

Gmail OAuth credentials stored by MailSentinel are encrypted before being saved. The encryption key is maintained separately from the stored credentials.

Temporary email files may be created during analysis. These temporary files are removed after processing is completed.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We take reasonable technical measures to protect information processed by MailSentinel.

## 6. Third-Party Services

Some MailSentinel functionality may use third-party services.

For example, public IP addresses extracted from email headers may be sent to an IP geolocation service to obtain geolocation information.

Only information required for that particular function is sent to the relevant third-party service. MailSentinel does not intentionally send the complete email message to the IP geolocation service for this purpose.

Google services are also used for Gmail authentication and read-only Gmail access.

Users should review the privacy policies of relevant third-party providers for information about how those providers process data.

## 7. Data Deletion

MailSentinel provides controls that allow an authenticated user to delete:

* Saved forensic case records associated with their MailSentinel account.
* Stored Gmail account credentials associated with their MailSentinel account.

Deleting these records does not erase blockchain transactions that have already been created.

Deleting MailSentinel's stored Gmail credentials also does not automatically revoke the application's authorization from Google. Users can revoke that authorization through their Google Account.

Deletion of data from operational backups or other systems, if applicable, may be subject to the retention practices of the deployed service.

## 8. Data Retention

MailSentinel retains account and forensic records for as long as they remain stored in the service or until the user deletes them using available deletion controls, subject to technical, legal, security, backup, and operational requirements.

Blockchain records created through MailSentinel may remain permanently because of the design of blockchain technology.

Specific retention periods may be updated as the service and its deployment infrastructure evolve.

## 9. Children's Privacy

MailSentinel is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law to use the service independently.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to providing the service.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when MailSentinel's functionality, data practices, or legal requirements change.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy will be updated.
